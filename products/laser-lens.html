Comprehensive Guide to Laser Protective Lenses

What is a Laser Protective Lens?

A laser protective lens acts as a sacrificial barrier in fiber laser cutting and welding machines. It prevents welding spatter, metallic dust, and fumes from damaging the expensive internal optics (like focusing lenses) of the laser head.

When Should It Be Replaced?

The 18x2mm laser protective lens should be inspected daily. You must replace it when you notice:

  • Visible burn marks, pitting, or spatter accumulation on the glass surface.
  • A decrease in welding power or an inconsistent beam spot.
  • Anomalous heating of the laser head housing.

Compatibility & Applications

Our 18x2mm lens is universally compatible with standard 1000W to 3000W handheld fiber laser welding and cleaning machines operating at a 1064nm wavelength. Ideal for automotive repair, metal fabrication, aerospace manufacturing, and precision engineering industries.

Cleaning & Maintenance Tips

If the lens is only lightly dusted, it can be cleaned using pure isopropyl alcohol or specialized optical cleaning fluid with a lint-free swab. Always wipe gently from the center outward to avoid scratching the anti-reflective coating.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these lenses compatible with all 1064nm lasers?

Yes, provided your laser head housing is designed to accept an 18mm diameter, 2mm thick optic. This size is standard across many leading fiber laser brands.
